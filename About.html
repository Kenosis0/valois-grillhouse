The story behind the flame

Respect the ingredient. Welcome the guest.

Valois Grillhouse began with a simple belief: great steak does not need to be complicated, just respected.

Our beginning

A neighborhood place with a long memory.

Valois was built around the rituals that make a meal feel lasting: a well-seasoned cut, a table set with care, and enough time to hear the whole story.

Our founder brought a respect for classic grillhouse cooking and a desire to make it feel at home in the neighborhood. Today, our kitchen keeps that promise through live-fire technique, thoughtful sourcing, and warm, unfussy service.
