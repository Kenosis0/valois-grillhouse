About Us: Valois Grillhouse

Welcome to Valois Grillhouse, where a deep-rooted passion for exceptional cuts meets the timeless art of live-fire cooking.

Founded on the simple belief that great steak doesn't need to be complicated—just respected—we pride ourselves on serving unforgettable meals in an atmosphere that feels both refined and comfortably rustic.
